Sawal jawab

Aksar poochhe jaane wale

Kya AI meri minimum price se neeche bech sakta hai?

Nahi — kabhi nahi. Prices AI set nahi karta; ek deterministic engine karta hai jo aap ki floor price ka paband hai. Upar se har outgoing message pe price-guard hai: AI koi aur number bole to message rukta hai aur ek safe template jata hai.

Paisa kaise milta hai? Aap commission lete hain?

Paisa seedha aap ko milta hai — COD pe delivery ke waqt, bank transfer pe aap ke apne account mein. Hum beech mein nahi aate aur koi commission nahi lete. Sirf mahana subscription.

Mera apna WhatsApp number use hoga?

Ji haan — aap ka apna business number Sahulatcart se connect hota hai. Customers ko wahi number nazar aata hai jo aap ke visiting card pe hai. Aap khud bhi kabhi bhi conversation take-over kar sakte hain.

Urdu ke ilawa aur kya samajhta hai?

Roman Urdu, English, aur voice notes mein Urdu/Punjabi/mixed. Jawab Roman Urdu mein deta hai — bilkul dukaandaar ke lehje mein.

Setup kitna mushkil hai?

10-15 minute: catalog upload karein (ya CSV import), rates aur limits set karein, WhatsApp connect karein. Hum setup mein poori madad karte hain — demo book karein.

Agar bot ko samajh na aye to?

Wo conversation aap ko de deta hai (human takeover) aur aap ko notification aati hai. Customer kabhi phansa nahi rehta — cancel, insaan se baat, ya payment method switch, sab keh sakta hai.
