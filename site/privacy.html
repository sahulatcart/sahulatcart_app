Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Sahulatcart provides an automated WhatsApp sales assistant to merchants in Pakistan. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and who it is shared with.

Two kinds of people are covered here. Merchants are our customers — the shop owners who sign up. Buyers are the people who message a merchant on WhatsApp. We process buyer data on the merchant's behalf; the merchant decides what their shop sells, what it charges, and how long they keep their records.

1. Data we collect

From buyers (via WhatsApp)

DataWhy
WhatsApp phone number and profile nameTo identify the conversation and route replies
Message content — text, voice notes, imagesTo understand the request and reply
Delivery name, address, area and cityTo create and fulfil the order
Payment screenshots (bank transfer)So the merchant can verify payment manually
Order and negotiation historyTo maintain the conversation and the merchant's records

From merchants

Business name, owner name, email address, login credentials, catalogue, pricing rules and bank account details shown to buyers who choose bank transfer.

2. How we use it

  • To operate the assistant: answer questions, negotiate within the merchant's limits, build orders, collect delivery details and confirm payment method.
  • To generate order slips and notify the merchant.
  • To let the merchant view and take over conversations from their admin portal.
  • To keep the service secure and diagnose faults.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use buyer conversations to advertise to them.

3. Automated processing

Replies are produced with help from an AI language model. Prices are not decided by the AI. All pricing and discount limits are computed by deterministic code from the rules the merchant sets; the model is used only to interpret messages and phrase replies. A buyer can ask to speak to a human at any point, and the merchant can take over the conversation.

4. Who we share data with

Each merchant can only access their own data. We do not share data between merchants.

5. Payments

We never take custody of money and we are not a payment processor. Orders are Cash on Delivery, or a bank transfer made directly to the merchant's own account. Payment screenshots are stored privately so the merchant can verify them; we do not process card or bank credentials.

6. Storage and retention

Data is stored in Supabase (Postgres and object storage) with access controls that isolate each merchant. Payment screenshots and inbound media are kept in private buckets and are not publicly accessible. Merchants control how long their order and conversation records are kept and may request deletion.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop messaging you. A buyer can stop the assistant at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp, which opts that number out of further messages.

Because we process buyer data on a merchant's behalf, requests about a specific shop's records may be passed to that merchant.

8. Children

The service is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The revision date at the top shows when it last changed.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent through our contact page.
