Kya karta hai

Ek salesman jo kabhi
chutti nahi karta

Customer ko lagta hai dukaandaar se baat ho rahi hai. Aap ko sirf notification aati hai: naya order aya hai.

🤝

Asli bhao-taao

Customers Pakistan mein bargain kiye baghair kharidte nahi. Sahulatcart counter-offers karta hai, "last price" bolta hai, aur kabhi aap ki minimum price se neeche nahi jaata. Engine deterministic hai — AI kabhi apni marzi ka rate nahi laga sakta.

🎤

Voice notes samajhta hai

Aadhe customers type nahi karte — voice note bhejte hain. Sahulatcart Urdu, Punjabi aur mixed voice notes sun kar samajhta hai aur usi waqt jawab deta hai. Transcript aap ke inbox mein bhi milta hai.

💵

COD ya bank transfer

Koi payment gateway nahi, koi commission nahi. Customer COD choose kare ya aap ke bank account mein transfer kar ke screenshot bheje — aap portal se ek tap mein verify karte hain. Paisa seedha aap ka.

🧾

Pakka order slip

Har confirm order pe customer ko PDF slip milti hai — items, rate, address, total, date aur time. Professional impression, kam disputes.

🧢

Khud upsell karta hai

Order confirm hote hi ek sasti cheez suggest karta hai — "sath mein cap sirf Rs 800?" Customer haan bole to address dobara nahi poochta. Basket size barhta hai, mehnat zero.

📊

Portal + analytics

Live inbox (aap kabhi bhi conversation take-over kar sakte hain), orders, catalog, payments verify, aur analytics — sab ek saaf-suthre dashboard mein, mobile pe bhi.
