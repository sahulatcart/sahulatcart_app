WhatsApp automation — common questions

What is WhatsApp business automation?

Software that answers your WhatsApp customers for you. With Sahulatcart that means the whole sale: answering product questions from your catalogue, bargaining within your price limits, collecting the delivery address, confirming the order and recording COD or bank transfer.

Is this the same as WhatsApp Business auto-reply?

No. The WhatsApp Business app can send a fixed greeting, an away message or a saved quick reply. It cannot read what the customer asked, quote a price, negotiate or take an order. Sahulatcart holds the whole conversation.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Sahulatcart connects your own WhatsApp Business number through the official WhatsApp Business Platform (often called the WhatsApp Business API). Customers keep messaging the number they already know.

Does the automation take payments?

No. Orders are Cash on Delivery or a bank transfer straight into your own account. The customer sends a screenshot and you verify it in the portal with one tap. Sahulatcart never holds or moves money.

Can I still reply myself?

Yes. Every conversation is visible in the merchant portal and you can take over any chat at any moment. Customers can also ask for a human and the assistant hands over.
