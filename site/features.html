Bhao-taao engine

AI baat karta hai.
Rate hum decide karte hain.

Sab se bara sawal: "AI ghalat rate laga de to?" Jawab: laga hi nahi sakta. Prices ek deterministic engine set karta hai — AI sirf jumla banata hai. Aur har outgoing message pe ek price-guard hai: agar AI koi aur number bole, message rukta hai.

  • ✦ Aap ki floor price sacred hai — min price, max discount %, ya margin guard
  • ✦ Concession curve — pehle thora, phir zyada, phir "last price"
  • ✦ Quantity samajhta hai — "3 kitnay ki?" pe per-piece aur total dono batata hai
  • ✦ Fuzool offers pe HOLD — Rs 150 wali offer pe curve nahi girti
Concession curve — Rs 2,500 shirt, floor Rs 2,000
Voice notes

Bolo, aur order ho gaya

Pakistani customers type kam, bolte zyada hain. Sahulatcart voice notes ko Roman Urdu mein transcribe kar ke usi tarah handle karta hai jaise likha hua message — bhao-taao, address, payment, sab voice se ho sakta hai. Transcript aap ke inbox mein bhi save hota hai.

Payments

Paisa seedha aap ka.
Hum beech mein nahi.

Koi wallet, koi gateway, koi commission nahi. COD orders slip ke sath confirm hote hain. Bank transfer pe customer aap ke account mein paisa bhej kar screenshot deta hai — aap portal mein dekh kar ek tap mein verify karte hain. Reject bhi kar sakte hain.

  • ✦ Customer beech mein COD pe switch kar sakta hai — order khud update ho jata hai
  • ✦ Cancel bhi keh sakta hai — aap ko notification milti hai
  • ✦ Har payment ka status: unpaid → claimed → verified
